Three files, a live preview, and suggestions that know which of the three you are typing in.

Things that are easy to miss

  • Numbers can be dragged. Put the caret on one and a grip appears under it. Hold Alt for tenths, Shift for tens.
  • Colours open a picker. Any hex, rgb() or hsl() value, in a stylesheet or an inline style.
  • Design makes the preview editable: click to select and jump to the markup, drag to move an element, pull the handles to resize it, Delete to remove it. Every gesture edits the source, and Ctrl+Z takes it back.
  • Class names cross over. CSS suggests classes your HTML uses; HTML suggests classes your CSS styles.
  • Save writes back to the files you opened, in place.
